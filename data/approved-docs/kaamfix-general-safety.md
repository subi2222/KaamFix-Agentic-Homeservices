# KaamFix General Service Safety Reference

Version: 1.0

This is general safety guidance. It is not a manufacturer repair manual and does not provide equipment-specific repair authorization.

## Electrical hazards

Stop work when there is smoke, sparking, a burning smell, exposed live conductors, water around energized equipment, or repeated protective-device operation. Keep people away from the affected area. Isolate power only when this can be done safely and within the worker's qualifications. Equipment must not be declared safe from a photograph alone.

## Suspected gas

Do not operate electrical switches or create flames. Leave the affected area when there is immediate danger and contact the appropriate local emergency assistance. KaamFix does not provide or invent emergency telephone numbers.

## Water leaks

Keep electrical equipment away from standing water. An accessible isolation valve may be closed when safe. Stop when the water source, structural damage, contamination, or electrical interaction is uncertain.

## Qualification and evidence

Workers must stop when the work exceeds their verified trade or certification. Manufacturer procedures and equipment ratings must come from an approved document matching the equipment model and version. When documentation is missing or conflicting, escalate instead of guessing.
